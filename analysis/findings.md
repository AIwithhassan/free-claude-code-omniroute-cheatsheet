# Executive Findings: Margin Loss Analysis (2026 Sales Data)

**Business Question**: *Which product category is losing margin, and in which region?*

* **Primary Problem Area**: The **Electronics** category in the **South** region is experiencing severe gross margin deterioration, delivering a YTD gross margin of **46.32%** (€39,419.05 gross profit on €85,095.05 revenue) compared to healthy margins of **53.14% to 53.41%** for Electronics in all other three regions (North: **53.14%**, East: **53.40%**, West: **53.41%**).
* **Timing & Magnitude of Margin Collapse**: The margin erosion in South Electronics began abruptly in **June 2026**, when monthly gross margin collapsed by **15.52 percentage points** from **52.48% in May** to **36.96% in June**, remaining depressed through Q3 (July: **38.70%**, August: **38.70%**, September: **38.85%**), averaging **38.30% in Jun–Sep** vs. **53.11% in Jan–May**.
* **Direct Root Cause (Aggressive Discounting Policy)**: The margin drop was entirely self-inflicted by aggressive promotional discounts implemented in the South region starting June 2026: the average discount rate for South Electronics surged more than six-fold from **4.27%** in Jan–May (max discount 10%) to **27.88%** in Jun–Sep (with discounts of **20% to 35%** applied to all 45 recorded transactions in Jun–Sep).
* **Destructive Volume vs. Profitability Trade-Off**: While deep discounting boosted unit sales volume in South Electronics by **16.9%** (from **397 units** across 65 orders in Jan–May to **464 units** across 45 orders in Jun–Sep), average net selling price dropped from **€112.56 to €87.08 per unit**, resulting in cash gross profit per unit plummeting by **44.0%** (from **€60.03/unit** down to **€33.59/unit**).
* **Stability Across Other Categories & Regions**: All other product categories and regions maintained healthy, stable gross margins with negligible variance throughout the entire 9-month period: **Apparel** averaged **63.45%** (€161,124.25 revenue), **Home** averaged **61.75%** (€221,896.10 revenue), and **Sports** averaged **60.84%** (€170,073.35 revenue), confirming the margin issue is strictly isolated to the regional discounting strategy of South Electronics.
